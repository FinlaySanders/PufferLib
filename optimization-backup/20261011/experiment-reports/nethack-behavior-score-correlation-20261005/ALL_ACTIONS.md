# Every logged action: score correlations

Forty 1B policies, eight five-policy blocks, seven unique ablation arms.
Each policy's value averages its 2,000 retained evaluation games.
Within = Pearson correlation after centering each five-policy block;
between = Pearson correlation of the seven arm means. These are
descriptive associations, not causal effects. MOVE, DOWN, UP, and KICK
never increment these counters, so they have no defined correlation.
Item-verb counters increment only after item selection succeeds. Rare verbs have
unstable coefficients. `q` is Benjamini-Hochberg FDR for the 22 active
verbs within that score/measure column, based on within-block shuffles.
