# Corrected offline variance study

No production training/engine/policy changes. No policy updates. Reuse the four frozen captures, but regenerate every target and refit every auxiliary predictor in this new directory.

1. Compile the unchanged production reward-clamp and GAE kernels from the pinned snapshot. Compare against an independently indexed scalar CUDA recurrence, bit for bit for BF16 advantages and returns. Cover synthetic clipping/terminal/horizon/importance cases and real streams.
2. Use those kernels to generate all targets, checking every stream. Captured reward/done follow the action; training stores them in the next observation slot. Clamp the total BF16 reward to [-1,1] before GAE. Gamma .999207735, lambda .484387815, horizon 512; same combined-done convention as the trainer. Use on-policy importance=1. Exclude 1,024 warmup actions and each horizon's final action, and retain unfinished episodes.
3. Examine all 12 item-using verbs. Models: per-verb state predictor (64/64 hidden units), state/item-key predictor, and directly centered state/item-key predictor. Targets are production GAE residuals. State-only and item-policy-averaged predictions are valid argument baselines. Item-centered predictions are valid controls for the verb head. All policy features remain frozen.
4. Fit on 192 whole odd-numbered environment streams, select checkpoints and coefficients on 64 other streams, audit on 256 even streams. Fix coefficients from the source validation split during transfer. Report 200 whole-game and whole-stream bootstrap intervals, conditional on fitted models, plus largest-game removal. No holdout-driven model selection.
5. Middle-checkpoint pilot, then all verbs at 131M, 524M, and 1B. Repeat the middle stage with another action seed; also evaluate 524M models at 1B without refitting. Repeat middle-stage auxiliary fitting with seed 29. Same training seed/checkpoint family throughout; this is not replication across independently trained RL policies.
6. Compare ordinary GAE, verb mean, gradient-optimal constant, verb-surprisal argument baseline, learned argument baseline, and item-Q marginal baseline. Verb controls: ordinary Q and directly centered Q. Coefficients for learned controls are clipped to [0,1]. Fit the optimal constant and coefficients using full decoder-gradient geometry on validation data.
7. Measure logit and full decoder-block gradient covariance traces. Item block includes query weights, shared key projection weights and temperature; verb block includes its linear weights. Exclude shared recurrent/encoder gradients, entropy/value gradients, optimizer transformations and off-policy PPO clipping. Recheck pointer derivatives against the source CUDA kernels on corrected-target subsets.
8. Promotion gate: at least 10% reduction on both middle action-seed audits, both game/stream upper 95% bounds below 1, and benefit survives largest-game removal. Check independent auxiliary initialization, constant controls and learning-stage dependence before recommending an online arm. No online experiments are authorized by this script.

The old reports remain explicitly invalid for the training objective. Old targets and fitted critics must never be loaded into this study. Only captured state/policy quantities are reusable.
