# NAO ascensions that did not enter Sokoban

This is a follow-up to `REPORT.md`. The xlog's Sokoban achievement bit means **collected the final prize**, not **never entered the branch**. I fetched final dumplogs for no-prize ascensions and required the final dungeon overview to contain no `Sokoban:` section. Of 474 no-prize ascensions, 391 dumplogs were available and 21 pass that stronger check; the 21 source URLs are in `no_sokoban_branch.csv`. These are examples of possible routes, not an estimate of the causal value of skipping Sokoban; the available-recording subset and its players are highly selected. All games below are 3.6.x, not NetHack 5.0.

The 21 Sokoban-free wins all used wishes (range 2–90; the 90-wish game is an extreme outlier). This is a property of this small selected set, not evidence that wishes are required to skip Sokoban. The two full recordings above show concrete ways to obtain reflection without the Sokoban amulet: dragon scales plus enchant armor. One carries a wished-for bag; the other ascends without a bag at all. The bag is useful for carrying a kit but is not a route prerequisite.

For an agent, the compact **candidate** route is: build a survivable early kit; obtain magic resistance and reflection from whatever the run offers (starting gear, dungeon loot, dragon scales, wishes); skip Sokoban when those needs are met; descend to Castle for its wand; then complete Quest, Vlad and Invocation. A bag can be opportunistic. These runs do not establish when an agent is strong enough to skip Sokoban or survive Castle, so compare skip/take policies under the same evaluation protocol. Wizard's starting magic-resistance cloak makes the route much easier than it is for a random challenge role.

Source limits: dumplogs show final inventory, level annotations, and visited branches, but not acquisition sequences. Only the StellaSchnella and WizGDQ entries above have a complete ttyrec checked against the final result. Do not infer “found on floor” from final possession or the absence of a Sokoban prize. The xlog cohort, filtering, and achievement definitions are in `REPORT.md` and scripts `01_ingest.py`–`06_report.py`.
