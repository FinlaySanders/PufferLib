# Persistent real-game armour intervention

Frozen 1B additive and replacement policies, eight independent trajectory seeds each, two arms per seed. Search up to 8,000 decisions; after the first eligible intervention, continue the natural policy for 128 decisions or until episode termination. Recurrent memory is retained and receives every subsequent real observation. Game dynamics and RNG advance normally. This is a diagnostic, not a performance certificate.

Eligibility: healthy (HP >=80%), not hungry, no condition bits, WEAR legal, worn armour publicly identified with known enchantment -4..4 and known noncursed, an eligible carried item in the same armour category. Engine additionally excludes any live hostile within Chebyshev distance eight (even unseen), swallowed/busy players, artefacts, unpaid items and non-singleton stacks. Safety screening is privileged experiment selection and not policy input. It holds at the intervention only; later threats are recorded.

Mutate the real carried object to the worn armour type, with matching erosion/proofness, known uncursed and fully known properties, and enchantment worn+3 versus worn-3. Preserve its object ID and inventory letter. Recompute object weight, refresh engine observations and rebuild the full policy observation/masks. The known type is already discovered from the worn item. Worn armour and player AC are unchanged until the policy acts. Both arms make identical changes except enchantment. This guarantees a six-point enchantment difference between arms without adding inventory clutter or changing item category. Control is a downgrade, not an unmodified trajectory.

The intervention is compiled as a separate object linked against the original frozen engine library. No engine binary, stock NLE, policy parameters or production source is modified. Track actual equipped state by object ID, rather than counting selected WEAR actions. A target that leaves inventory and later returns retains its ID. Terminal auto-reset is treated as the end of follow-up. Object identity across the paired intervention is asserted in analysis.

Limitations: few independent trials, selected armour situations, possible later threats, artificial item transformation without an explanatory game message, and a fixed 128-decision window. Lack of equipping does not by itself prove that wearing was optimal at every point. Report both immediate probability and eventual equipment use.
